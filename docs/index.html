Bio

I’m an applied economist and research software engineer working on causal inference and AI tooling for economic data. I hold a Master’s in Applied Economics from the Institute of Economics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, where my thesis traced whether Santiago’s recent Metro expansion — Lines 3 and 6 and the Line 2 extension — actually reaches local government finances through property and business taxes. Before that, I built data infrastructure for evidence-based policy at Peru’s Ministry of Education.

My current focus is building Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers that let LLMs query Latin American and global statistical databases directly, instead of researchers hand-scraping them.

Research & Technical Expertise

Open Source | Creator and maintainer of mcp-bcrp, mcp-wbgapi360, and mcp-sinim for AI access to central bank, World Bank, and Chilean municipal data, plus enahodata and usebcrp for Python and Stata.

Master’s Thesis | Traced whether Santiago’s Metro expansion reaches local government finances — a 6.7% residential price premium near Line 3 stations, but weak translation into property tax revenue. Institute of Economics, PUC Chile.

## Bio

I'm an **applied economist** and **research software engineer** working on causal inference and AI tooling for economic data. I hold a Master's in Applied Economics from the **[Institute of Economics](https://economia.uc.cl) at [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl)**, where my thesis traced whether Santiago's recent Metro expansion — Lines 3 and 6 and the Line 2 extension — actually reaches local government finances through property and business taxes. Before that, I built data infrastructure for evidence-based policy at Peru's **[Ministry of Education](https://www.gob.pe/minedu)**.

My current focus is building **[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io)** servers that let LLMs query Latin American and global statistical databases directly, instead of researchers hand-scraping them.
